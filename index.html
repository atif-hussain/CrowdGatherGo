01 · Long-distance rail

Five-minute slots. Five-hundred trains a day. Same tracks.

Indian Railways doesn't need new corridors to carry more — it needs analytical timetables. We treat every 1-km train as a virtual slot running at a uniform speed with a fixed 5-minute headway. Express and slow trains coexist by overtaking at stations — the slow one waits on the loop while the express sails past on the main line. Result: corridor capacity expands without laying a single new kilometre.

  • Slot-based scheduling — every train picks the next available 5-minute slot. FCFS, deterministic, simulatable.
  • Loop-line overtakes — eliminate the express-vs-passenger speed conflict that wastes capacity today.
  • Open freight wagons with suburban stops — cars, bikes and containers roll on, multimodal by default.
  • Feedback-stabilised runs — analytics flag drift early and keep the corridor self-correcting.

02 · City interchanges

Most jams are made at junctions — not on roads.

Every traffic-stream model (Greenshield, Greenberg, Underwood) tells the same story: flow peaks at half the jam density, then collapses. Once you cross that point, congestion cascades into a jam that takes hours to clear. We keep cities on the jam-free side of that curve — with small, surgical changes to junctions, not billion-dollar flyovers.

  • Identify high-density corridors — Pareto holds: 80% of volume runs on under 20% of roads.
  • Access-control them — lane dividers, controlled entries/exits, no sudden lane-switching.
  • Replace 4-way signals with U-turn pairs, butterfly loops, or signal-light cloverleafs based on geometry.
  • Pilot in 1 week — paint, signage and signal-timing tweaks; measure before/after; then scale.

03 · Last-mile crowd connectivity

Buses are great. Bus-stops are the problem.

Buses lose capacity not on the road, but at the stop — slow boarding, single doors, queueing at terminals. Our patent-pending road-wagon + platform system fixes the dwell-time bottleneck: multi-door wagons sync with raised platforms so people flow on and off in seconds. You get light-rail-grade volumes at bus-grade cost — and crucially, on existing roads.

  • Low dwell time — multi-door, level boarding from platforms, ARAI Type-1 compliant.
  • Road-sharing — no dedicated corridors needed; cheaper than median/curb BRT, deployable anywhere.
  • Built for surge — office-area peaks, inter-modal connections, Hajj / Kumbh / national processions.
  • Inter-modal glue — connects railway stations, airports and bus-stands at the throughput they actually need.
