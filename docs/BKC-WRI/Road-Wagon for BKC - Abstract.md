## Abstract

BKC hosts ~200,000 daily commuters arriving at three transit anchors — Bandra (Western Line), Kurla (Central / Harbour), and the new BKC Metro on Line 3 — yet the last 1.5 km to the office plots remains a private-vehicle problem, choking BKC's internal road loop every weekday.

We propose deploying CrowdGatherGo's patent-pending Road-Wagon — a 12 m battery-electric multi-door high-floor bus (1 stepped nearside door + 6 platform-level doors, 92-passenger, ARAI Type-I DLX) — across two complementary services on existing BKC roads:

- **R1 Perimeter Loop (~4.4 km, 9-min roundtrip):** a circulator hitting every major office cluster along BKC's outer ring.
- **R2 Spine Connector (~5.4 km, 12-min trip):** a shuttle linking BKC Metro ↔ Bandra Station ↔ Kurla Station ↔ Eastern Express Highway.

At 90-second headways, each route delivers ~3,700 pphpd — light-rail-grade throughput using modular 1.1 m raised platforms installable overnight, with no dedicated corridor and no civil cutover.

Mini-Pilot: 2 buses, 2 platforms, 30 days, ₹6cr. 
R1-pilot: 6 buses, 8 platforms, 90 days, ₹40cr capex.
Scale-up: 14-bus fleet in 18 months, removing ~10,000 private-vehicle trips daily.
Extensibility: doubling platforms support 96 buses on R2, serving up to 22,000 pphpd, matching current average BKC footfall.
